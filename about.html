Our Promise

My Story — From Passion To Purpose

Music found me early. From primary school, I knew I loved music. When I got to secondary school, I started learning it seriously — and I passed very well.

That passion carried me to Embu University, where I pursued my Degree in Music. It was not just about passing exams. It was about understanding music deeply — so I could teach it to others.

Today, I serve as a tutor in a secondary school and as a trainer in my church. I have seen first-hand how music changes students: it builds confidence, discipline, worship, and joy.

I created Melody Academy because I know many people cannot afford to pay universities, and many others are doing other courses but still want to learn music. This platform is for them. For you.

“If you have passion for music, you deserve a teacher who will walk with you — step by step. That is why Melody Academy exists.” — Dennis Gitonga
